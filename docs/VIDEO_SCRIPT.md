# Demo video script — Harvester Risk Atlas

Use the web atlas at https://harvester-risk-atlas.vercel.app. The spoken core is approximately 60–90 seconds; confirm the organiser's submission limits before recording.

## Script

**Overview**

“This is Harvester Risk Atlas. It helps us ask which wild medicinal plants of the Philippines need conservation attention first, where they grow, and how sure we are. The app, pipeline, methods and documentation were developed on November 2–3, 2026.”

**Atlas and plant profile**

“On the atlas, select a plant or an area. Locations are grouped into broad hexagonal areas. Each ranked plant combines three equally weighted parts: rarity, sensitivity to harvesting, and habitat exposure. Climate exposure is shown separately and has no weight in the default score.”

**Evidence and uncertainty**

“Open a plant profile to see why it ranks here, its sources, and how far its rank could move. Plants with insufficient records or that are not native are listed without a score. Curated values awaiting expert review are labelled.”

**Field brief and How it works**

“The field brief is one printable page. How it works explains the method, lets you try different weights, and shows the robustness checks and limitations. Public data, images and libraries are credited, and AI assistance is disclosed.”

**Close**

“This is a research and education tool, not medical advice, a harvest permit or a Red List assessment.”

## Recording notes

- Follow Overview → Atlas → plant profile → field brief → How it works.
- Use the visible ranking and evidence rather than assuming a particular plant's rank or status.
- Keep secrets and account settings off camera.
- Keep the development dates consistent with [`ORIGINALITY.md`](ORIGINALITY.md).
