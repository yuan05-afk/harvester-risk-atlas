## Description

```
Up to 4 billion people rely on herbal medicine, and most medicinal plants in trade are taken from the wild. Harvester Risk Atlas is a free, open map that ranks which wild medicinal plants of the Philippines need conservation attention first, where they grow, why, and how sure we are.

EthnoHack 2026 · Track 3: Biodiversity & Sustainability
Team: Yuan Andrei C. Mariano and Alicia Jhyle Vilale

▶ Try it: https://harvester-risk-atlas.vercel.app
▶ Code, data and method: https://github.com/yuan05-afk/harvester-risk-atlas
▶ Slides: https://github.com/yuan05-afk/harvester-risk-atlas/blob/main/submission/Harvester-Risk-Atlas-slides.pdf

Chapters

02:03 Limits, and try it

How it works: for each of 29 native medicinal plants, the Harvest Priority Index combines rarity (corrected for survey effort), harvest harm (which part is taken, life form, sourcing) and habitat pressure (protected-area gap, forest loss). The ranking is re-run 2,000 times with different weights and evidence, so every plant gets a rank range and a tier. Plants appear only as ~250 km² hexagons, never as exact points.

Data: GBIF occurrence records; World Checklist of Vascular Plants (Kew); WDPCA Philippines (UNEP-WCMC); tree-cover loss (Hansen et al. 2013, Global Forest Watch); WorldClim 2.1 and CMIP6; Stuart, Philippine Medicinal Plants; DENR DAO 2026-20 (validation only). Plant photos from iNaturalist observers, credited in the app. Problem figures: IUCN Medicinal Plant Specialist Group and TRAFFIC, "Wild at Home" (2018); Kew, State of the World's Plants and Fungi (2020).

For research and education. Not medical advice, not a harvest permit, and not a Red List assessment. Harvest traits are drawn from published sources and still await expert verification.

Narration: an ElevenLabs synthetic voice ("Jennica") reading the team's script. Names are checked against World Flora Online, and plants against Co's Digital Flora of the Philippines (Pelser, Barcelona & Nickrent, eds., 2011 onwards). Footage is the live web app. AI assistants helped build the project; see the repository's ORIGINALITY.md.
```
